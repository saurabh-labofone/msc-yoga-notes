Research papers for MSYSMN-104(1) Application of Hatha Yoga & Patanjali Yoga Sutra

Checked on PubMed on 6 Nov 2026. Findings come from the abstracts only. PRF = Patanjali Research Foundation. S-VYASA and PRF are marked only where the PubMed affiliation shows them. Used in sem-1/104-hatha-yoga-sutra/index.html.

Corrections found during the check: the Sharma VK cognition paper is 2014 (the 2013 paper is on perceived stress). The Deshpande Gunas paper is 2009. The Telles 2013 children abstract does not name Haridwar schools.

Pranayama and nadis

Paper PMID Design Finding
Bernardi L et al. 2001. Rosary prayer and yoga mantras on autonomic cardiovascular rhythms. BMJ 11751348 n = 23 Breathing slowed to 6/min. Baroreflex sensitivity 9.5 → 11.5 ms/mmHg.
Telles S, Sharma SK, Balkrishna A 2014. BP and HRV during alternate nostril breathing. Med Sci Monit Basic Res (PRF) 25408140 n = 26 + 15 Systolic BP fell. RMSSD and NN50 rose.
Sharma VK et al. 2013. Fast and slow pranayama on perceived stress and CV parameters. Int J Yoga 23930028 RCT, 90, 12 wk Both cut stress. Only slow pranayama lowered HR and DBP.
Sharma VK et al. 2014. Fast and slow pranayama on cognitive functions. J Clin Diagn Res 24596711 RCT, 84, 12 wk Both improved executive function, stress and reaction time.
Dinesh T et al. 2015. Slow and fast pranayama on pulmonary function. Int J Yoga 25558130 RCT, 91 PEFR and FEF25-75 up. FVC and FEV1 not significant.
Telles S, Nagarathna R, Nagendra HR 1994. Nostril breathing and metabolism. Indian J Physiol Pharmacol 8063359 48 men O2 use +37% right nostril.
Raghuraj P, Telles S 2008. Nostril-manipulating breathing. Appl Psychophysiol Biofeedback 18347974 Crossover, 21 Alternate nostril lowered BP. Right nostril raised BP.
Yadav R et al. 2024. Bhramari and Om chanting in asthmatic children. J Asthma 37788160 RCT, 110 Asthma controlled 68.2% vs 38.5%.
Nataesh SR et al. 2025. Uddiyana bandha, BP and HRV. J Bodyw Mov Ther 41316586 Crossover, 30 Vagal dominance at 10 min. Diastolic BP rose right after.

Shatkarma

Paper PMID Design Finding
Swathi et al. 2021. Narrative review of shatkarma studies 33454186 Review Closest source. No direct trial of shatkarma for stress found.
Head K et al. 2018. Saline irrigation for allergic rhinitis. Cochrane 29932206 14 studies, 747 Symptoms SMD −1.32. Low quality.
Yoder JS et al. 2012. PAM deaths after sinus irrigation with tap water. Clin Infect Dis 22919000 2 deaths Safety: use boiled, distilled or filtered water.

Stress and anxiety

Paper PMID Design Finding
Schleinzer A et al. 2024. Yoga on stress in stressed adults: SR/MA. Front Psychiatry 39553891 13 RCTs, 1,026 SMD −0.69 short term. Low quality.
Thirthalli J et al. 2013. Cortisol and antidepressant effects of yoga. Indian J Psychiatry 24049209 Open-label, 54 Cortisol fell more often with yoga.
Cramer H et al. 2018. Yoga for anxiety: SR/MA of RCTs. Depress Anxiety 29697885 8 RCTs, 319 SMD −0.43 vs no treatment. No effect in DSM anxiety disorders.
Telles S, Gaur V, Balkrishna A 2009. Yoga practice vs theory session on state anxiety. Percept Mot Skills (PRF) 20178291 n = 300 Anxiety −14.7% practice, −3.4% theory.

Attention, cognition, children

Paper PMID Design Finding
Gothe NP, McAuley E 2015. Yoga and Cognition: MA of chronic and acute effects. Psychosom Med 26186435 15 RCTs + 7 acute g = 0.33. Attention 0.29, executive 0.27.
Talwadkar S, Jagannathan A, Raghuram N 2014. Trataka on cognitive functions in the elderly. Int J Yoga (S-VYASA) 25035618 RCT, 60 Better letter cancellation and Trail Making B.
Telles S et al. 2013. Yoga or exercise in children: RCT. Child Adolesc Psychiatry Ment Health (PRF) 24199742 RCT, 98 Within-group gains. Only social self-esteem differed, favouring exercise.
Manjunath NK, Telles S 2001. Tower of London after yoga. Indian J Physiol Pharmacol 11881575 RCT, 20 girls Planning time −53.9% to −59.1%.

Meditation, aggression, compassion

Paper PMID Design Finding
Lazar SW et al. 2005. Meditation and cortical thickness. Neuroreport 16272874 Cross-sectional, 20 Thicker prefrontal cortex and right insula.
Hölzel BK et al. 2011. Mindfulness and grey matter density. Psychiatry Res 21071182 16 vs 17 Grey matter up in hippocampus, PCC, TPJ.
Fox KC et al. 2014. Meditation and brain structure: MA. Neurosci Biobehav Rev 24705269 21 studies 8 regions, d = 0.46. Publication bias concern.
Deshpande S, Nagendra HR, Raghuram N 2008. Yoga on verbal aggressiveness. Int J Yoga 21829289 RCT, 226 Less verbal aggression than exercise (P = 0.013).
Bilderbeck AC et al. 2013. Yoga in a prison population. J Psychiatr Res 23866738 RCT, 100 Less distress, better response inhibition.
Lv J et al. 2020. Four Immeasurables meditations on depression: MA. Clin Psychol Rev 31945711 21 RCTs, 1,468 d = 0.38.
Kirby JN et al. 2017. Compassion-based interventions: MA. Behav Ther 29029675 21 RCTs, 1,285 Compassion 0.55, depression 0.64, anxiety 0.49.

Work, sport, personality, digital addiction

Paper PMID Design Finding
Della Valle E et al. 2020. Workplace yoga and perceived stress: MA. J Funct Morphol Kinesiol 33467249 6 studies −0.67 vs no treatment.
Telles S et al. 2018. Residential yoga in teachers. Med Sci Monit Basic Res (PRF) 30061552 n = 236 Well-being up, state anxiety down.
Polsgrove MJ et al. 2016. Yoga, flexibility and balance in college athletes. Int J Yoga 26865768 n = 26 Only yoga group improved.
Bühlmayer L et al. 2017. Mindfulness and sports performance: MA. Sports Med 28664327 9 trials, 290 Psychological 0.72, shooting/darts 1.35.
Deshpande S, Nagendra HR, Nagarathna R 2009. Yoga on Gunas and self-esteem. Int J Yoga (S-VYASA) 21234210 RCT, 226 Sattva up, Tamas down vs exercise.
Amaranath B, Nagendra HR, Deshpande S 2016. Yoga and personality of home guards. J Ayurveda Integr Med (S-VYASA) 27297509 RCT, 148 Sattva 39.87 → 47.14, Tamas 30.98 → 25.40.
Fendel JC et al. 2024. Mindfulness for problematic internet use: MA. J Behav Addict 38703362 19 RCTs + 20 g = −1.67 in RCTs. Low quality.
Rao NS, Bhat R, Sharma MK 2026. Yoga in adolescents with Internet Gaming Disorder. Front Public Health (S-VYASA) 41846846 RCT, 120 Symptoms, addiction and stress fell.
