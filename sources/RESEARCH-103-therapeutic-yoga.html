Research papers for MSYSMJ-103 Therapeutic Yoga

Checked on PubMed on 6 Nov 2026. Findings come from the abstracts only. S-VYASA and Patanjali Research Foundation are marked only where the PubMed affiliation shows them. Used in sem-1/103-therapeutic-yoga/index.html, Unit 4.

1. Cardiovascular

Paper PMID Design Finding
Manchanda SC et al. 2000. Retardation of coronary atherosclerosis with yoga lifestyle intervention. J Assoc Physicians India 11273502 RCT, 42 men with CAD, 1 yr More lesions regressed (20% vs 2%), fewer progressed (5% vs 37%). Revascularisation 1 vs 8.
Prabhakaran D et al. 2020. Yoga-Based Cardiac Rehabilitation After Acute Myocardial Infarction. J Am Coll Cardiol 32241371 Multicentre RCT, 3,959 Events 6.7% vs 7.4% (HR 0.90, not significant). Self-rated health better.
Chu P et al. 2016. Yoga and risk factors for CVD and metabolic syndrome: SR/MA. Eur J Prev Cardiol 25510863 MA, 37 RCTs BMI −0.77, SBP −5.21 mmHg, LDL −12.14 mg/dL. No HbA1c change. Equal to exercise.
Pullen PR et al. 2008. Yoga, inflammation and exercise capacity in chronic heart failure. J Card Fail 18514933 RCT, 19 Exercise time +18% vs −7.5%. IL-6 and hsCRP fell.

Not used: Ornish 1990, Lancet (PMID 1973470). Its abstract says "stress management", not yoga.

2. Respiratory

Paper PMID Design Finding
Yang ZY et al. 2016. Yoga for asthma. Cochrane 27115477 15 RCTs, 1,048 AQLQ +0.57. FEV1 change not significant. Moderate quality.
Nagarathna R, Nagendra HR 1985. Yoga for bronchial asthma: a controlled study. Br Med J (S-VYASA) 3931802 Matched, 53 vs 53 Fewer attacks, lower drug scores, better peak flow.
Yadav R et al. 2024. Bhramari pranayama and Om chanting in asthmatic children. J Asthma 37788160 Open-label RCT, 110 Asthma controlled 68.2% vs 38.5%. Lung function unchanged.

3. Neurological

Paper PMID Design Finding
Kumar A et al. 2020. Yoga as add-on therapy in migraine (CONTAIN). Neurology 32376640 Open-label RCT, 160 Headache frequency fell more with add-on yoga (difference 3.53).
Kwok JYY et al. 2019. Mindfulness yoga vs stretching and resistance training in Parkinson disease. JAMA Neurol 30958514 RCT, 187 Equal on motor symptoms, better on anxiety and depression.
Panebianco M et al. 2017. Yoga for epilepsy. Cochrane 28982217 2 trials, 50 Low quality. No reliable conclusions.
Lawrence M et al. 2017. Yoga for stroke rehabilitation. Cochrane 29220541 2 RCTs, 72 Very low quality. No clear benefit.

4. Psychiatric

Paper PMID Design Finding
Cramer H et al. 2013. Yoga for depression: SR/MA. Depress Anxiety 23922209 12 RCTs, 619 SMD −0.69 against usual care.
Janakiramaiah N et al. 2000. Sudarshan Kriya Yoga in melancholia vs ECT and imipramine. J Affect Disord 10708840 RCT, 45 Remission: ECT 93%, imipramine 73%, SKY 67%.
Varambally S et al. 2012. Add-on yogasana in stabilised schizophrenia. Indian J Psychiatry 23226845 RCT, 3 arms About 5× more likely to improve on negative symptoms.
Streeter CC et al. 2010. Yoga vs walking on mood, anxiety and brain GABA. J Altern Complement Med 20722471 RCT, 19 vs 15 Mood and anxiety better with yoga, linked to thalamic GABA.
Goyal M et al. 2014. Meditation programs for psychological stress and well-being. JAMA Intern Med 24395196 MA, 47 RCTs, 3,515 Anxiety 0.38, depression 0.30, pain 0.33. Not better than active treatments.

5. Endocrine and metabolic

Paper PMID Design Finding
Nidhi R et al. 2013. Holistic yoga and endocrine parameters in adolescent PCOS. J Altern Complement Med (S-VYASA) 22808940 RCT, 90 AMH, LH, testosterone, hirsutism fell more than with exercise.
Nilkantham S et al. 2025. Yoga module and quality of life in hypothyroidism. J Med Internet Res (S-VYASA) 40570327 RCT, 134 All SF-36 domains improved (P<.001).
Cui J et al. 2017. Yoga in type 2 diabetes: MA. J Diabetes Investig 27370357 12 RCTs, 864 HbA1c −0.47%. Fasting glucose −23.72 mg/dL. High heterogeneity.
Telles S et al. 2014. Yoga vs walking in overweight and obese adults. Med Sci Monit (Patanjali) 24878827 RCT, 68, 15 days Both cut BMI and waist. Yoga also lowered LDL. Within-group tests only.

6. Renal

Paper PMID Design Finding
Yurtkuran M et al. 2007. Modified yoga exercise in haemodialysis patients. Complement Ther Med 17709061 RCT, 37 Pain −37%, fatigue −55%, urea −29%, creatinine −14%.
Nair RG et al. 2026. Yoga in lifestyle-related CKD. Ann Behav Med (S-VYASA) 42847350 RCT, 81 Creatinine −0.42, eGFR +6.94 against usual care.

7. Reproductive

Paper PMID Design Finding
Kim SD 2019. Yoga for primary dysmenorrhoea: MA. Complement Ther Clin Pract 31383452 4 RCTs, 230 Pain SMD −2.09. Only four trials.
Cramer H et al. 2018. Yoga for menopausal symptoms. Maturitas 29452777 13 RCTs, 1,306 Total symptoms SMD −1.05 vs no treatment.
Narendran S et al. 2005. Yoga and pregnancy outcome. J Altern Complement Med (S-VYASA) 15865489 Matched, 335 More babies ≥2500 g, less preterm labour, less IUGR.

8. ENT and eye

Paper PMID Design Finding
Head K et al. 2018. Saline irrigation for allergic rhinitis. Cochrane 29932206 14 studies, 747 Symptoms SMD −1.32. Low quality.
Chanta A et al. 2022. Hatha yoga in allergic rhinitis. Asian Pac J Allergy Immunol 31421665 RCT, 27 Nasal flow up, symptoms down.
Yoder JS et al. 2012. Amebic meningoencephalitis deaths after sinus irrigation with tap water. Clin Infect Dis 22919000 2 deaths Safety: use boiled, distilled or filtered water for neti.
Udenia H et al. 2021. Pranayama and diaphragmatic breathing for IOP in glaucoma. J Glaucoma 33955942 RCT, 90, 6 mo IOP 20.85 → 14.90 mmHg. Adjunct only.
Dada T et al. 2018. Mindfulness meditation reduces IOP in glaucoma. J Glaucoma 30256277 RCT, 90, 21 days IOP 18.8 → 12.7 mmHg. Cortisol fell.
Sankalp et al. 2022. Tratak and IOP in glaucoma. Int J Yoga 35444375 RCT No difference. Not recommended for raised IOP.
Jasien JV et al. 2015. IOP during four yoga positions. PLoS One 26698309 10 + 10 Safety: head-down poses raise IOP (17 → 28 mmHg in glaucoma).
Baskaran M et al. 2006. IOP during Sirsasana. Ophthalmology 16806478 75 Safety: IOP doubled.

9. Healthy volunteers and specific practices

Paper PMID Design Finding
Telles S, Nagarathna R, Nagendra HR 1994. Nostril breathing, metabolism and autonomic activity. Indian J Physiol Pharmacol 8063359 Randomised, 48 men O2 use +37% right, +24% left, +18% alternate nostril.
Raghuraj P, Telles S 2008. Nostril-manipulating breathing: autonomic and respiratory variables. Appl Psychophysiol Biofeedback 18347974 Crossover, 21 Alternate nostril lowered BP. Safety: right nostril raised BP.
Telles S, Singh N, Balkrishna A 2015. High-frequency yoga breathing. Med Sci Monit Basic Res (Patanjali) 26270968 Crossover, 47 + 20 O2 use and energy rose during, then back to baseline.
Streeter CC et al. 2007. Yoga asana and brain GABA. J Altern Complement Med 17532734 Pilot, 19 GABA +27% after one session.
Telles S et al. 1995. Autonomic changes during Om meditation. Indian J Physiol Pharmacol 8582759 7 meditators Heart rate fell.
Ross A, Thomas S 2010. Health benefits of yoga and exercise: review. J Altern Complement Med 20105062 Review, 81 studies Yoga equal or better except fitness.
Nataesh SR et al. 2025. Uddiyana bandha, BP and HRV. J Bodyw Mov Ther 41316586 Crossover, 30 Vagal dominance at 10 min. Safety: diastolic BP rose right after.

No good mudra study found (only a small quasi-experiment on Brahma mudra).
