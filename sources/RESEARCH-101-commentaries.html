Claim Verdict Correct wording Source
Vachaspati's works: Bhamati, STK, NV-tatparya-tika, TV, Nyayakanika, Tattvabindu Corrected Seven works, named by him at the end of the Bhamati. Add Tattva-samiksha (on Vedanta, now lost). His Nyayasucinibandha is also extant. Woods 1914 pp. xxi–xxiii

Bhikshu on Ishvara and kaivalya Confirmed Safest line: Bhikshu is more openly theistic than Vachaspati (Ishvara is the ground of prakriti and purushas, and bhakti is a path), and he accepts jivanmukti before final release. Dasgupta I p. 223 ("more inclined to theistic Samkhya or Yoga"); Dasgupta III pp. 450, 489

Claim Verdict Correct wording Source
20a. Vachaspati on YS 1.17 (with 1.41): vitarka, vichara = grahya; ananda = grahana; asmita = grahitri Confirmed Vitarka and vichara rest on objects. Ananda rests on the sense organs. Asmita has the knower as object. Woods 1914 pp. 40–41; Jha 1907, notes to YS 1.17
20b. Does Bhikshu read ananda and asmita differently? Confirmed Yes. Bhikshu rejects this scheme. Ananda's object is bliss itself, not the senses. Asmita's object is the bare self as "I". Vachaspati counts 8 samadhis; Bhikshu counts 6. Dasgupta 1924 pp. 153–154

Corrections the notes must make

  1. Vachaspati wrote seven works, not six. Add Tattva-samiksha (lost). Source: Woods 1914 pp. xxi–xxii.
  2. YS 1.48: "bears only truth, no trace of error" is Vyasa's Bhashya. Do not give it to Vachaspati. Source: Woods 1914 p. 94.
  3. HYP 2.12 (Pancham Sinh): the third stage is "steadiness", not "rising". Source: Pancham Sinh 2.12.
  4. HYP sapada-koti verse: write 4.65 if you cite Pancham Sinh (not 4.66).
  5. HYP "hatha without raja is fruitless": write 4.78 if you cite Pancham Sinh (not 4.79).
  6. Nada stages: the Rudra granthi breaks at nishpatti (4.75), not at parichaya. Parichaya names no granthi. Pancham Sinh gives only "flute" for nishpatti.
  7. The 196-sutra count: do not name an edition. No checked source names one. Woods counts 195, and editions differ at YS 3.20, 3.21 and 4.16.
  8. Vachaspati's date: write "disputed, 841 CE or 976 CE". Do not pick one.
  9. Make these more exact: HYP "about 1400 CE"; Vyasa "about 400 CE"; Ramanuja "traditional 1017–1137 (recent view 1077–1157)"; Shankara "8th c. (700–750 or 788–820)".
  10. YS 2.4: add that Vyasa counts the burnt-seed state as a fifth state.
  11. "Each soul is potentially divine": in the 1896 first edition it stands before the Preface. In the Complete Works it closes the Preface.
  12. Jaakkola 2020 has no PMID. Cite the DOI.
  13. Bhikshu's "avibhaga-advaita": cite it as "a type of bhedabheda" (Dasgupta III p. 471). The label itself was seen only on Wikipedia.
