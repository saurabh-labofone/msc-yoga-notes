Exam pattern (UoP, same from 2022 to 2024)

  • 3 hours · 70 marks · questions in Hindi and English.
  • Section A: 5 long questions × 15 marks. Answer any 3 = 45 marks.
  • Section B: 7 short questions × 5 marks. Answer any 5 = 25 marks.
  • The 2026 syllabus gives 75 final marks, so the split can change a little. Not confirmed.
  • Old long questions often come back as short questions, and the reverse. Some questions repeat word for word.
