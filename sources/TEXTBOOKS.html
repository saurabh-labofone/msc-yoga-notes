Where to get the Semester 1 textbooks — legal sources only

Checked 6 Nov 2026. Every link loaded on that date unless a note says otherwise.

The short answer: almost every syllabus book is in copyright. There is no legal free PDF of the Divya Prakashan books (Swami Ramdev, Acharya Balkrishna), Gore, Bhalekar, Tortora, the Muktibodhananda HYP, Iyengar, Knudson, or the Hindi music books. Buy them, or borrow them from the university library. Pirate copies are not listed here.

The good news: many classical texts have old public-domain English translations, and free open textbooks and open-access research papers cover most of the rest.

MSYSMJ-101(P) — Practicum

Ramdev Yoga Sadhana evam Yoga Chikitsa Rahasya, Pranayam Rahasya, Balkrishna Yoga Vijnanam — all in copyright, buy. Yoga Vishwakosh is not published yet. Free help: Common Yoga Protocol, Pancham Sinh HYP ch. 2–3, Gheranda Samhita.

MSYSMJ-102 — Anatomy & Physiology

Gore, Bhalekar (Kaivalyadhama: https://kdham.com/kdhamproducts/product/anatomy-physiology-yogic-context/), Tortora, Khurana, Pearce — all in copyright. Free substitute: OpenStax A&P 2e.

MSYSMJ-103 — Therapeutic Yoga

All 7 text books and 10 reference books are in copyright. Free substitutes: the open-access reviews in row 9 above + the research list in RESEARCH-102-anatomy-physiology.md.

MSYSMN-104(1) — Hatha Yoga + Yoga Sutra

MDNIY's HYP is not a free download (MDNIY's list shows only priced titles). Iyengar, Devpriya — in copyright. Free: Pancham Sinh HYP, Woods 1914, Vivekananda Raja Yoga, Gheranda Samhita. The Rieker HYP link in the syllabus (hermetics.org) is dead (404), and that translation is in copyright.

MSYSMN-104(2) — Biomechanics

Hay & Reid, Knudson — in copyright. Free: OpenStax A&P 2e ch. 9–11 · OpenStax College Physics 2e §9.6 "Forces and Torques in Muscles and Joints": https://openstax.org/books/college-physics-2e/pages/9-6-forces-and-torques-in-muscles-and-joints

MSYSID-105(3) — Classical Music

All the Hindi music books (Sinha, Srivastava, Shrivastava, Joshi, Sangeet Prashnottar) are in copyright. No free legal copy found. Buy: https://www.amazon.in/s?k=Raag+Parichay+Harishchandra+Srivastava Bhatkhande's own text is public domain in India (he died in 1936). One scan of Kramik Pustak Malika Book 2 (Bombay 1946) is on archive.org, but its legal status is not confirmed: https://archive.org/details/dsnn-hindusthani-sangeet-paddhati-kramik-pustakmalika-d. The current Hathras editions carry the editor's copyright.

MSYSRRA-106 — Research Review

Balkrishna & Yadav books — in copyright. Free: ICMR 2017 guidelines, PRISMA, STROBE, PROSPERO, OpenIntro Statistics.
